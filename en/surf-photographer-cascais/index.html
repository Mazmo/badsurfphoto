Surf photographer / Cascais

Surfing in Cascais, shot from the beach with a telephoto lens

BAD SURF PHOTO is based in Parede, in Cascais. That makes Carcavelos, Guincho and Praia Grande our usual spots, with no travel cost, and lets us pick the best time with the tide and wind of the day.

Carcavelos is the easiest for beginners and lessons, Guincho is for fuller waves and wind, and Praia Grande, near Sintra, has peaks for several levels. We photograph individual surfers, groups of up to 5, lessons and schools.
