Surf schools and camps

Surf lesson, surf school and surf camp photos

Most people who learn to surf in Portugal fly home without a single photo of themselves in the water. A friend's phone on the sand is not enough: too far away, too shaky, and usually pointed at the wrong surfer.

If you are taking a lesson or a camp

I shoot from the beach with a telephoto lens during your lesson or your surf camp week. I do not teach and I do not replace the instructor — I only handle the images. You get edited files within 48 hours, with vertical crops ready for Instagram.

If you run a surf school or camp

I can cover your sessions through the week and hand you a consistent image archive for your website, your social media and your guests to take home. New content every week, without anyone on your team putting down a board to film.

Prices per hour

  • Solo photography — €40
  • Solo video — €50
  • Solo photo + video — €60
  • Group photography, up to 5 — €70
  • Group video, up to 5 — €90

Editing included. Usual areas: Carcavelos, Guincho, Praia Grande, Costa da Caparica and Ericeira. Schools and camps with regular sessions get their own rates — just ask.
