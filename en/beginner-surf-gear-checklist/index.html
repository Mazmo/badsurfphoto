Basic surf equipment for beginners

To start surfing you do not need much kit, but what you need has to be right. This is the checklist I would give a friend.

The essentials, in order of importance

I see many beginners on the beach with the wrong kit: a board that is too small, a worn leash, no sun protection. You do not need to spend a lot, but there are items worth investing in, especially those that affect safety and comfort.

Board, leash and fins

The board is the centre of it all. To begin with, a soft-top or funboard with plenty of volume is usually the easiest starting point; we have a guide on how to choose a surfboard. The leash links the board to your ankle and should be roughly the length of the board, in good condition, with a firm velcro strap. The fins must fit properly in their boxes and be tightened securely.

Wax and deck pad

To avoid slipping off the board you need grip: wax applied where you stand, or a deck pad, which is an adhesive traction mat. Wax comes in versions for colder and warmer water; choose according to the sea temperature. Many soft-top boards already have a non-slip surface and do not need wax.

Wetsuit, boots and rash vest

In Portugal a wetsuit is almost essential, in a thickness that varies by season: we have a dedicated guide. In summer, a rash vest protects you from the sun and from board rash. On colder days, boots and a hood make the session much more pleasant.

Sun protection and extras

Use waterproof sun cream, especially on your face, ears and the back of your neck, and reapply. A hat or cap for before and after the session helps, as do sunglasses for the beach. A water bottle, a snack and a big towel for changing are simple but very useful extras.

Bag and transport

A board bag protects the board from knocks and sun, which extends its life. A waterproof bag or bucket helps you carry a wet wetsuit home. Never leave the board in the sun inside a car: the heat ruins it fast.

FAQ

Quick questions.

What do I really need for my first session?

A board, leash, a wetsuit suited to the season and sun protection. If you have a lesson with a school, the basic kit is usually sorted by them.

Do I need wax?

If the board has no non-slip surface, yes. On many soft-tops it is not needed.

How long should the leash be?

As a rule, about the length of the board. Ask someone who knows your gear.
