Coming to surf in Portugal?

Photos and video of your session, shot from the beach — and a quick guide to plan the trip.

Your session

Photos and video of your surf session

Every year thousands of people fly in to surf the Lisbon coast. They take a lesson or a few sessions, catch the best waves of the trip — and fly home without a single photo of themselves in the water. BAD SURF PHOTO does only that: surf photography and video. We do not teach lessons, run camps or rent boards.

Who it is for

Surfers of any level, on their own board or a rented one, visiting or living here; and anyone taking a lesson or a surf camp who wants an independent record of it. Groups of up to 5 surfers are covered too.

How it works

  • Talk to us — tell us the area, likely date, your level and whether you want photo or video.
  • We read the ocean — we confirm spot and time close to the date, based on the swell, wind and tide forecast.
  • How we spot you — before you paddle out we note your wetsuit and board colours and the peak you will surf.
  • Shooting — from the beach with a telephoto lens, so we never get in the way of your surf. In groups we shoot everyone and organise the images by surfer.
  • Delivery — edited high-resolution files by private link, usually within 48 hours, with vertical crops ready for Instagram.

Photo or video

Photography delivers every usable wave of the session, selected and edited. Video delivers clips of your waves, stabilised and organised for analysis or editing. You can ask for one or both.

Prices (per hour, plus VAT, editing included)

  • Solo photography — €40/hour
  • Solo video — €50/hour
  • Solo photo + video — €60/hour
  • Group photography, up to 5 — €70/hour
  • Group video, up to 5 — €90/hour

Sessions usually last 1 to 2 hours. If there are no waves, we reschedule at no cost. Arrange it on WhatsApp or through the form, in English or Portuguese.

Planning the trip

Board, wetsuit, beach and season

Board and wetsuit

You do not need to bring gear: surf schools and beach shops in Carcavelos, Costa da Caparica, Ericeira and Peniche usually rent boards and wetsuits by the hour, the day or the week, and will advise the right volume for your level. We do not rent or sell equipment. Portugal is a wetsuit country all year round: thinner in summer, thicker from November to April.

Where to surf, by level

  • First time or little experience — Carcavelos, Foz do Lizandro, Baleal and Costa da Caparica: sandy bottoms and softer peaks.
  • You already surf — Praia Grande, Ribeira d’Ilhas and the peaks around Ericeira.
  • You surf well — Guincho with clean swell, Coxos and Supertubos.
  • Just to watch — Nazaré in winter, from the fort, is one of the most impressive sights in Portugal.

When to come

September to November is a good time: water still warm, consistent swell and fewer people than in August. Winter brings the biggest waves. Summer is the easiest for beginners.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does BAD SURF PHOTO teach lessons or rent boards?

No. It is an independent surf photography and video service. We do not teach, run camps or rent or sell equipment.

Do I need to bring a board and a wetsuit?

No. Surf schools and beach shops in Carcavelos, Costa da Caparica, Ericeira and Peniche usually rent boards and wetsuits by the hour, the day or the week. Portugal is a wetsuit country all year round.

How much does it cost to get photos of my session?

Solo photography €40/hour, solo video €50/hour, photo and video together €60/hour, group photography up to 5 people €70/hour and group video €90/hour, plus VAT. Editing is included.

What if there are no waves on the day?

We confirm the day before based on the swell, wind and tide forecast. If conditions do not work, we reschedule at no cost.

When do I get the photos?

Usually within 48 hours, by private link, in high resolution and with vertical crops ready for Instagram.

Do you shoot bodyboard, longboard or SUP?

Yes. It is the same job: if you are in the water, I shoot you from the beach with a telephoto lens.

Which beaches do you shoot?

Regular areas: Carcavelos, Guincho, Praia Grande, Costa da Caparica and Ericeira, with no travel cost. Peniche, Baleal, Supertubos and Nazaré are possible with travel agreed before booking.
