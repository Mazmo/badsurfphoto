Surf photography in Portugal

Surf photography and video, shot from the beach

BAD SURF PHOTO does surf photography and video in Portugal. I shoot your session from the beach with a telephoto lens, without getting in the way of your surf, and deliver edited images by private link, usually within 48 hours. The regular areas are Cascais (Carcavelos and Guincho), Sintra (Praia Grande), Ericeira and Costa da Caparica; Peniche and Nazaré on request.

It is for surfers of any level, on their own board or a rented one, for groups of up to 5 and for anyone taking a lesson or a surf camp. Photography from €40/hour and video from €50/hour, plus VAT, editing included. We do not teach or rent equipment: photography and video only.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does surf photography in Portugal cost?

At BAD SURF PHOTO, solo photography is €40/hour, solo video €50/hour, photo and video together €60/hour, group photography up to 5 people €70/hour and group video €90/hour, plus VAT. Editing is included.

Where do you shoot surf photography?

The regular areas are Cascais (Carcavelos and Guincho), Sintra (Praia Grande), Ericeira and Almada (Costa da Caparica). Peniche, Nazaré and other beaches are possible with travel agreed before booking.

How do I get the photos of my session?

Usually within 48 hours, by private link, in high resolution and with vertical crops ready for Instagram. If you need a quick selection to post the same day, tell us when booking.
