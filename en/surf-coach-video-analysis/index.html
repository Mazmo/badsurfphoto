Surf coaching: how to choose a coach and how video helps

A good coach helps you progress faster. And video of your session gives both of you something concrete to analyse instead of vague memories.

Surf guide / Coaching

What a coach can do for you

Surfing without feedback is like trying to fix your own golf swing with your eyes shut: you feel something is off, but you cannot see what. A surf coach, with a trained eye, sees your foot position, the timing of your rotation, how you use the rails and which waves you choose, and turns that into simple things to work on.

How to choose a coach

Look for someone with experience and recognised training who can explain what they see in simple words. Ask how they work: on land, in the water, with video? Ask for references from students at your level and see whether the coach adapts the plan to your goals, whether that is competing, improving your style or building confidence in bigger waves. A good coach does not promise miracles.

What to ask a coach

Ask for a clear plan with only a few points at a time. Instead of ten corrections, ask for two or three things you can work on until the next session. Ask for feedback soon after the session, and for the reason behind it, not just the what. And agree on goals: catching more waves, improving your paddling, a solid bottom turn, a controlled cutback.

How video helps

You see yourself from the outside, and that changes everything. On video you can check where your knees are, where your head is, whether your back shoulder rotates too early and at what point of the wave you do the manoeuvre. You can slow it down, compare sessions and confirm whether a correction worked. The coach benefits too, with material to show and to keep over the months.

How to prepare a video session

Tell the photographer what you want to see: paddling, take-off, bottom turn, manoeuvres. Pick a day when the sea lets you work on what you want. Arrange it with your coach, if you have one, so they can watch the video the same day. Wear a wetsuit or rash vest in a contrasting colour that stands out in the water, which makes analysis easier.

No. We do surf photography and video. The video can be used by you and your coach to analyse your technique.
