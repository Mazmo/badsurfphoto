Surf event photography / Portugal

Surf event coverage from the beach

At a contest, three people want the pictures at once: the athlete who wants their heat, the organiser who needs content for social media, and the sponsor who wants to see the brand. We shoot from the beach with a telephoto lens and sort the material by heat and by athlete, so everyone finds theirs quickly.

The service is imaging only: we do not organise events or judge heats. Our usual areas are Cascais, Sintra, Ericeira and Almada; Peniche, Nazaré and other beaches need travel agreed before booking.

FAQ

Quick questions.

Do you cover surf contests?

Yes. We photograph and film tournaments, school contests and surf meets in Portugal, with delivery through a private link.

How is an event priced?

For one-day events the price starts from our hourly rates: photo €40/h and video €50/h, plus VAT. For larger events, send the dates and the number of heats on WhatsApp and a quote follows the same working day.

Can athletes buy their own photos?

Yes. Each athlete receives the images of their heat through a private link, and team photos can be requested too.
