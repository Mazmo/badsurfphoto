Privacy Policy

A simple explanation of what data we handle, why, and what your rights are. Last updated: 29 October 2026.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Maurício Zebini Moura, who provides surf photography and video services under the name BAD SURF PHOTO, in Portugal. Privacy contact: badsurfphoto@gmail.com.

What data we handle and why

When you contact us through WhatsApp, Instagram or email, we handle your name, contact details and the details of your request (date, area, type of session) to reply and book the session. The legal basis is your request to contact or hire the service.

During sessions we photograph and film surfers in the water from the beach. Images are delivered by private link to whoever booked the session. We only publish them on social media or on the site when that was agreed, or when they are photos of public events, and you can ask for an image of you to be removed.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses no advertising cookies and only stores data when you request a session at /marcar/ (Portuguese): name, phone, email and request details, used only to handle that request. It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and measure page speed, without cookies and without identifying individual visitors. We also count, in aggregate and without storing IP addresses or personal data, how often the WhatsApp, email and booking buttons are clicked and how many forms are started and sent (only the day, page, language and type of source, such as “Google” or “direct”). The infrastructure that serves the site (Cloudflare) may process visitors' IP addresses to deliver pages and protect the site against abuse.

Who we share it with

We do not sell data. We use third-party services to communicate and deliver work, such as WhatsApp, Instagram, email and file storage. Links to these services and to Google Maps lead to sites with their own policies.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and files for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or erasure of your data, object to processing or ask to restrict it, by writing to badsurfphoto@gmail.com. If you believe the processing breaks the law, you can complain to the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD, cnpd.pt).
