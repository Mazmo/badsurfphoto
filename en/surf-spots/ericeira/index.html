Surf photographer in Ericeira: how it works

Ericeira is a World Surfing Reserve with enough peaks to find a wave in almost any swell. Ribeira d’Ilhas, Foz do Lizandro and Coxos are minutes apart, so we can change peak without losing the session.

I shoot from the beach with a telephoto lens, so nothing changes for you: surf as usual and I take care of the rest. We agree on the spot and time the day before, based on the swell, wind and tide forecast.
